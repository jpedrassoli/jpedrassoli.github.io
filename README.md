# Julio Pedrassoli, Ph.D.

Geographer blending remote sensing and social justice to map cities, risks and resilience in the Global South.

- Assistant Professor — Department of Geography, University of São Paulo (FFLCH-USP)
- Adjunct Professor — School of Environment, Geography and Sustainability, Western Michigan University
- Coordinator of the urban mapping team — MapBiomas

## Research

Satellite data, cloud computing and machine learning to monitor how cities change: urban expansion, favelas and precarious settlements, urban vegetation, and exposure to climate risk, with a focus on tropical and Global South cities.

## Education

- Ph.D. in Geography — University of São Paulo
- M.S. in Remote Sensing — University of São Paulo
- B.S. in Geography — São Paulo State University
