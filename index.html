01Research

My research combines satellite data, cloud computing and machine learning to monitor how cities change — where they grow, who lives in precarious settlements, and how urban populations are exposed to climate risk. I develop open, scalable methods for mapping urban areas, favelas and urban vegetation, with a focus on tropical and Global South cities.

  • Remote sensing
  • Slum & favela mapping
  • Urban land cover change
  • Climate risk in cities
  • Spectral mixture models
  • Google Earth Engine
